5 FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Jewel Loop – Coloring Sort free?

Yes. It is free to download, with ads and optional in-app purchases (including ad removal).

Is there a timer?

The mobile game has no countdown — you play at your own pace, but you must watch the loop capacity.

How do I play?

Tap a group of connected same-color jewels. They travel around the loop and fill matching cells on the pixel canvas. Fill the whole picture to win.

Which platforms are supported?

iPhone and iPad (iOS 13+), Apple-silicon Macs, Android and Windows via Google Play.

Are finished pictures saved?

Yes — every completed artwork goes into your collection album.
